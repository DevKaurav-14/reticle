### Changed

- **`@reticlehq/core`, `@reticlehq/browser`, `@reticlehq/server` — the Harness no longer refuses "Start free trial" as "would need to pay".** Three of four explores failed on it: the Harness read paying from a button's words, so "Start free trial", "Upgrade", "Subscribe" and "Place order" were all refused on the app under test. The payment boundary is now where money can move. A Harness drive whose journey does not ask to pay refuses only to type into a card or bank field (autocomplete `cc-number`, `cc-csc`, `cc-exp`, or a name or label that says card number, CVC, expiry or IBAN) and to open a payment provider's origin (Stripe, Dodo Payments checkout, Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, PayPal, Razorpay, Braintree), by a link, a form or `reticle_navigate`. Your own agent's calls and saved-flow replays are not gated, as before.
